How the forecasting script works

Twenty-six steps, grouped into five stages. Click any step to read what it does, why it is there, and what can go wrong.

1 · You uploadA spreadsheet of dates and values. Nothing is computed yet.
2 · It reads the shapeWhich column is the date, which is the value, and the cadence.
3 · You chooseWhich models to run and how far ahead to project.
4 · One pass runsDiagnostics, macro drivers, then three models fitted and scored.
5 · You compareAll three judged on unseen periods. Download the numbers.
